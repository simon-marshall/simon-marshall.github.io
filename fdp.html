Meals

Standing rules

  • Olive oil: 1 level tablespoon per portion.
  • Dry pasta: 75 g per portion.
  • Vegetables: two fists per meal minimum. Add more freely.
  • Parmesan: 15 g per portion. Real parmesan, and worth it.
  • Salt, pepper, garlic, herbs, chilli, lemon, vinegar — unlimited, and used aggressively.

Every recipe is written for one portion. The columns scale it:

ColumnMeaningMultiply by
DDinner tonight, alone×1
D+LDinner tonight + lunch tomorrow×2
D2+LDinner for two + lunch tomorrow×3.5

For whole items — eggs, aubergines, tins — round up rather than down.

Monday — Pasta e ceci

75 g ditalini or broken spaghetti · 120 g drained chickpeas (½ tin) · 1 tbsp olive oil · 1 garlic clove · 1 sprig rosemary · 1 tbsp tomato purée · 300 ml vegetable stock · 15 g parmesan · pinch of chilli

DD+LD2+L
Pasta75 g150 g260 g
Chickpeas½ tin1 tin1¾ tins
Stock300 ml600 ml1 L
Olive oil1 tbsp2 tbsp3½ tbsp

Warm the oil with sliced garlic, rosemary and chilli — gently, don't colour the garlic. Tomato purée in, cook one minute. Chickpeas and stock, simmer 10 minutes. Blend about a third with a stick blender; this is what makes it creamy without cream. Add the pasta and cook it in the soup, loosening with water as it tightens. Rosemary out, parmesan and a thread of oil in.

Reheats beautifully but thickens overnight — loosen with a splash of water.

Friday — Homemade pizza

Per base: 130 g '00' flour · 85 ml water · 2 g dried yeast · 3 g salt · 1 tsp oil
Per pizza: 80 g passata, raw, with just salt and oil · 80 g fior di latte, drained well and torn · basil

Dough in the morning or the night before, risen in the fridge. Oven as hot as it goes with a tray or stone preheating inside. 8–10 minutes.

Two bases each if wanted, loaded with vegetables — roasted peppers, courgette, artichokes, olives, rocket after baking.

Slice the aubergine 1 cm thick, salt for 20 minutes, pat dry. Brush with oil — brush, don't pour — and roast at 220°C for 20–25 minutes until golden. Meanwhile simmer the passata with garlic and basil. Layer sauce, aubergine, mozzarella, parmesan, and repeat. Bake 30 minutes at 180°C. Rest 15 minutes before cutting or it collapses.

Sunday — Lentil ragù with tagliatelle

60 g dry brown or green lentils · 1 small carrot · 1 celery stick · ½ onion · 1 tbsp olive oil · 200 g passata · 1 tbsp tomato purée · bay leaf · splash of red wine · 75 g tagliatelle · 15 g parmesan

Soffritto finely chopped, in the oil on low for 12–15 minutes until sweet. Purée, cooked out. Wine, let it evaporate. Lentils, passata, bay, and 500 ml water per two portions. Simmer 35–40 minutes until the lentils are soft and the sauce thick. Season hard at the end.

Make the double batch and freeze half in portions — that covers Week 2's Sunday lunch with no work at all.

Saturday — Torta salata with leek, ricotta and egg

Makes 4 slices — bake the whole thing and treat it as two dinners, or dinner plus two lunches.
250 g ricotta · 2 eggs · 2 large leeks · 40 g parmesan · 1 sheet shortcrust · 1 tbsp olive oil · nutmeg · thyme

Leeks sliced and softened in the oil with the lid on for 15 minutes until melting, with no colour. Cooled slightly, then mixed with the ricotta, eggs, parmesan, nutmeg and thyme. Into the lined tin, 190°C for 35–40 minutes until set and golden.

One quarter per portion, with a large green salad alongside.

Training

Three sessions a week, 45–55 minutes each including warm-up and stretching. Full-body, machines throughout.

Every session

  • Warm-up, 5 minutes — stair machine or brisk incline walk
  • The lifts — as listed below
  • Optional finisher, 10 minutes — stair machine, if time allows
  • Stretch, 5 minutes

Day A

  • Leg press — 3 × 10
  • Chest press machine — 3 × 10
  • Seated cable row — 3 × 12
  • Lateral raise (light dumbbells) — 3 × 12
  • Dead bug — 3 × 10 each side

Day B

  • Lat pulldown — 3 × 10
  • Romanian deadlift (dumbbells) — 3 × 10
  • Chest-supported row or machine row — 3 × 12
  • Face pulls (cable, rope attachment) — 3 × 15
  • Plank — 3 × 30 seconds

Day C

  • Hip thrust or glute bridge machine — 3 × 10
  • Shoulder press machine — 3 × 10
  • Seated row — 3 × 12
  • Leg curl — 3 × 12
  • Reverse fly (machine or dumbbell) — 3 × 15

Picking the weight

Pick a weight where the last two reps are hard but form holds. When all three sets can be completed at the top of the rep range, add the smallest available increment next time. Keep a note of exercise, weight, sets and reps on your phone.

Away from the gym

A long resistance loop band (about £10) lives permanently in the travel bag. Two 25-minute sessions across the weekend, plus a long walk each day.

  • Band rows
  • Band face pulls
  • Band pull-aparts
  • Goblet squats holding a loaded rucksack
  • Glute bridges
  • Push-ups against a kitchen counter
  • Walking lunges
